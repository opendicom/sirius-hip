Sirius HIP - /studyToken URL builder is a tool to help you build and test the different options provided by this method. Keep in mind that Sirius HIP must be previously configured correctly as well as the different viewers that use it such as OHIF, Weasis or OsiriX. If you are trying to configure Sirius HIP or any Dicom Viewer for the first time, please go to the configuration section in the online documentation.

Options allowed to test:

  • Custom filters for study selection like SOPClassOff, ModalityOff, etc.
  • Download requested study in a zip archive built on the fly.
  • Launch OHIF DICOM Viewer to view the requested study, series, or instance.
  • Launch Weasis DICOM Viewer to view the requested study, series, or instance.
  • Launch OsiriX DICOM Viewer to view the requested study, series, or instance.
Wildcards: use * (any sequence) and ? (one char). Without wildcards, values are treated as exact matches.
Auth (when enabled): use Authorization: Bearer <token>. For browser-based testing, you may include token=... in the URL.
